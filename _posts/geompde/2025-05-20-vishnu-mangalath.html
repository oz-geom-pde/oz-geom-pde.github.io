--- layout: post title: A contracting geometric flow of arbitrary initial surfaces related to MCF speaker: Vishnu Mangalath profile-pic: vishnu-mangalath.jpg speaker-url: https://maths.anu.edu.au/people/vishnu-mangalath speaker-inst: - ANU category: geom-pde-reading-seminar-season06 youtube-id: zKlxLEKV-rQ abstract: We introduce a new extrinsic geometric flow whose speed is given by a positive function of the mean curvature. This flow retains several desirable features of mean curvature flow (MCF) in the mean convex setting yet imposes no restrictions on the initial surface. Notably, we show that blow-up limits are convex ancient solutions to MCF. We also discuss certain limitations of the flow, such as the absence of a noncollapsing, and suggest a possible strategy to overcome this issue. ---
