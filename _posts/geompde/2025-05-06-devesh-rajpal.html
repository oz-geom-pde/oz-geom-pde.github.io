--- layout: post title: Affine boosted Gauss curvature flow speaker: Devesh Rajpal profile-pic: devesh-rajpal.jpg speaker-url: speaker-inst: - ANU category: geom-pde-reading-seminar-season06 youtube-id: -qwE_3gFuPE abstract: We introduce an affine boosted version of the alpha-Gauss curvature flow for sub-affine powers alpha less than 1/(n+2). A new ingredient is the convexity of the associated Firey and Gaussian entropy in the affine orbit due to which we can do affine corrections. It can be proven that the new flow converges convex hypersurfaces to a point. The bounded monotonic entropies for affine boosted flow with bounded isoperimetric ratio further suggest convergence to new self-similar solutions for sub-affine exponents. This is joint work with Ben Andrews. ---
