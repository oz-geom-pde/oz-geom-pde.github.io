--- layout: post title: Harmonic-Einstein metrics on Riemann surfaces and Higgs bundles speaker: Adam Thompson profile-pic: adam-thompson.jpg speaker-url: speaker-inst: - UQ category: geom-pde-reading-seminar-season06 youtube-id: qIb1-uBuCIc abstract: I will talk about the harmonic-Einstein equation, a coupled system of equations for a harmonic map between Riemannian manifolds and a Einstein-like metric on the domain. This system is of independent interest, but also arises in the study of Ricci solitons (and Einstein metrics) with symmetry. I will focus on the case the domain is a Riemann surface and explain a link with Higgs bundles. This is based on joint work with R. Lafuente. ---
