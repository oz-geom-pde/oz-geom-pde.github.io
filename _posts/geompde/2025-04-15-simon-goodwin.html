--- layout: post title: Killing tensors, prolongation and tractors in conformal geometry speaker: Simon Goodwin profile-pic: blank.jpg speaker-url: speaker-inst: - Uni Aukland category: geom-pde-reading-seminar-season06 youtube-id: obNdVn9PyPI abstract: Conformal Killing tensors are solutions of a natural conformally invariant weakening of the projectively invariant Killing equation from Riemannian geometry. The Killing equation is an overdetermined PDE, and is naturally studied via prolongation. A projectively invariant prolongation of the Killing equation for any rank has been developed using projective tractor calculus. Recently, a differential prolongation of rank two conformal Killing tensors has also been developed, utilising the invariant conformal tractor calculus. We explore these methods and give an overview of prolongation and tractors. We then extend the results obtained in previous papers, specifically to Ricci flat manifolds, and discuss new applications to superintegrable systems. ---
