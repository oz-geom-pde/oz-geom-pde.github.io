--- layout: post title: New invariant metrics and a counterexample to the Kobayashi-Wu conjecture speaker: Kyle Broder profile-pic: kyle-broder.jpg speaker-url: https://www.kylebroder.com/ speaker-inst: - UQ category: geom-pde-reading-seminar-season06 youtube-id: QG-GBc8Ye2E abstract: An old conjecture of S. Kobayashi and H.-H. Wu predicts that all hyperbolic complex manifolds admit a (possibly singular) invariant Hermitian metric with negative holomorphic curvature. This is shown to be false, even for arbitrarily singular metrics. I will also talk about an infinite family of new invariant metrics that exhibit strong negative curvature properties for a certain class of domains. This is joint work with Florian Bertrand and Hervé Gaussier. ---
