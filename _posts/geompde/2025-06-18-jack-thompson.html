--- layout: post title: Density and growth of s-stationary sets speaker: Jack Thompson profile-pic: jack-thompson.jpg speaker-url: speaker-inst: - UQ category: geom-pde-reading-seminar-season06 youtube-id: 3nfVJxA05BQ abstract:

Since the celebrated 2010 article by Caffarelli, Roquejoffre, and Savin, the analysis of nonlocal perimeters and nonlocal curvature has become an incredibly active area of research. While these objects share several properties with their classical counterparts, they also exhibit striking differences, making their study both rich and challenging.

In this presentation, I will discuss two of my recent results concerning the density and growth of sets with zero nonlocal curvature. The first result provides a lower bound on the perimetric density, which, as an application, yields a fractional analogue of the Michael–Simon Sobolev inequality. The second result is a half-space theorem in the plane.
